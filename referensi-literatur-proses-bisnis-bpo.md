# Referensi Literatur — Daftar End-to-End Proses Bisnis Industri Outsourcing/BPO

> Bahan referensi literatur generik tentang industri outsourcing/BPO secara umum — bukan bagian dari log temuan audit.

**Pre-Sales & Contracting**
Business Development/Client Acquisition, Bid Management & Proposal, Contract Negotiation & SLA Definition, Client Onboarding/Account Setup, Pricing & Rate Card Management

**Workforce Planning & Sourcing**
Manpower/Workforce Planning & Demand Forecasting, Recruitment & Selection, Sourcing & Talent Pool Management, Background Verification/Screening, Offer Management

**Onboarding & Deployment**
Employee Onboarding & Induction, Employment Contract & Documentation Management, Statutory Registration (jaminan sosial, pajak), Deployment/Placement to Client Site, Asset & Equipment Provisioning

**Workforce Operations**
Time & Attendance Management, Shift & Roster Scheduling, Leave & Absence Management, Replacement/Backfill Management (reliever/buffer), Performance Management & Appraisal, Training & Development, Employee Relations & Grievance Handling, Discipline & Corrective Action Management, Occupational Health & Safety/Incident Management

**Payroll & Finance**
Payroll Processing, Tax Compliance & Reporting, Employee Benefits Administration, Billing & Invoicing, Accounts Receivable/Collections, Cost Allocation & Profitability Analysis

**Client & Contract Management**
Client Relationship/Account Management, SLA Monitoring & Performance Reporting, Contract Renewal & Amendment Management, Client Complaint/Dispute Handling

**Compliance & Governance**
Regulatory Compliance Management, Industrial Relations & Dispute Resolution, Data & Document Governance, Quality Assurance/Audit Management, Sub-Contractor/Vendor Management

**Employee Self-Service & Support**
Employee Self-Service/HR Helpdesk, Client Self-Service Portal, Internal Communication & Announcement Management

**Offboarding & Post-Employment**
Termination/Exit Management, Final Settlement & Severance Processing, Alumni Management/Rehire Pool, Post-Employment Obligations (referensi kerja, dokumen pajak akhir)

**Analytics & Continuous Improvement**
Workforce Analytics & Reporting, Client Health/Account Risk Scoring, Process Improvement & Change Management
